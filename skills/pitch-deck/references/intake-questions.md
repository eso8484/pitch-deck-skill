# Intake questions for founders without a codebase

Ask only what the plan, website or earlier answers didn't already cover. Group the questions into two or three rounds at most. Always say "skip anything you don't know yet". Plain language: these founders may not be technical.

## Round 1: the core (needed for every deck)
1. **Name and one-liner.** What is the company or product called, and how would you describe it to a friend in one sentence?
2. **Who it's for.** Who is your customer? Be specific (e.g. "owners of small hair salons in Lagos", not "businesses").
3. **The problem.** What frustrates them today, and how do they solve it now (spreadsheets, a competitor, nothing)?
4. **Your solution.** What does your product do for them? What are the 3–4 main things it does?
5. **Audience for this deck.** Who will see it: investors, a grant or accelerator, a bank, customers, partners? Is there a deadline or a slide limit?
6. **Stage.** Idea, building, launched, or making money? Is there a website or an app people can use today?

## Round 2: the business (ask what the audience needs)
7. **Making money.** Who pays, how much, and how often (subscription, one-off, commission, ads)? Planned prices are fine; say they're planned.
8. **Proof so far.** Anything real to show: sign-ups, waitlist, pilots, paying customers, revenue, letters of intent, awards, press? Numbers only if you're happy for them to be on a slide.
9. **Competition.** Who else solves this, and why will customers pick you?
10. **Market.** How many potential customers are there, and in which countries or cities? Any reports or sources you trust?
11. **Why now.** What changed recently that makes this the right time (technology, regulation, behaviour)?

## Round 3: people, the ask and the look
12. **Team.** Who is behind it? Names, roles, and one relevant fact each (only what you want shown). Photos optional.
13. **The ask.** What do you want from the audience: an amount of money, a pilot, a partnership, a grant? What would the money be used for?
14. **Next milestones.** What will you achieve in the next 6–12 months?
15. **Brand.** Do you have a logo (send the file or a link) and brand colours? A tagline? If not, I'll choose a clean look and show you.
16. **Visuals.** Any mock-ups, designs, product photos or Figma files I can use?
17. **Contact.** What should go on the last slide (email, website, phone)?

## How to use the answers
- Turn answers into short, benefit-led headlines; don't paste them verbatim.
- Mark plan-only features as planned or in development, never as live.
- Numbers the founder gives go on slides only if they said they can be shown. Market numbers need a source; if they have none, research one and cite it, or leave the number out.
- Anything still unknown goes on the "facts to confirm" list at hand-over, not on a slide as `[TBD]`.
